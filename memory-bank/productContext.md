# Dark Forest Engine — Product Context

## 1. The Inverted Progression Loop
The player's role inverts traditional game progression:
- **Phase 1 (The Ground State):** Visceral survival in the Dark Forest as a physical survivor or faction commander navigating the 14-day Shift, managing Runic Barrier Fuel, 50-ton anchor chains, and squad survival.
- **Phase 2 (The Ascended State / Crow's Eye):** Macro-observation, anomaly investigation, symbolic Dream interventions, and manipulating macro-causality as an Editor of Reality (`PUBLISH`, `LEAK`, `SELL`, `ARCHIVE`, `SUPPRESS`, `DESTROY`).

## 2. The 6 Playable Mobile / Commander Roles
In the Mobile General Ledger (`ledger-mobile/` & `src/core/commander_engine.js`), the player leads one of six asymmetrical factions through a **Squad Management $\rightarrow$ Resource Management $\rightarrow$ Base Upgrade $\rightarrow$ End-of-Day Reconciliation** loop:
1. **Noble House Scion (`NOBLE_HOUSE`):** Commands 1 of 18 competing Noble Houses, managing canonical monopolies (Ironwood, Runic Dust, Siphon-Vine Resin, Leviathan Deep-Oil, etc.), Crown Fuel Tithes, Autarky Stills, and the 19-House Feudal War Board.
2. **Terminus Warden (`TERMINUS`):** Commands World's End Gate and the 300 Guardians with legal Horizon Dispensation and 3-Stage Bone-Carving Cultivation.
3. **Adventurer Guild & OCP Directorate (`GUILD` / `ORDER`):** Contains spatial anomalies, files Form 12-B reports, and suppresses Blind-Spot Entropy before reality leaks expose the 21st Settlement (*Epitaph*).
4. **Merchant Network & Minor Divine Patron (`MERCHANT` / `PATRON`):** Executes commodity arbitrage across settlements, verifies historical truths, and whispers symbolic dreams to elevate mortal candidates.
5. **Under-Chain Crime Syndicate (`SYNDICATE`):** Smuggles contraband resin, ambushes Noble House convoys, and extorts Salt-Denier tolls at the Gallows-Gate Sluices.
6. **The Pale Sovereign (`SOVEREIGN`):** Plays as Korvan the Variable (The First Wendigo) in the Mountain Ring, dispatching the Bound Huntsman, performing the Macabre Communion to mutate 5 Herald Wendigos, and enduring 1,000 years to shatter his chains.

## 3. Core UX & Gameplay Principles
- **The Hostility of Acquisition (Manifesto §5):** Every expedition is a march, not a timer. Resource generation is subject to Logistical Entropy Taxes and route hostility.
- **The Will to Live (Manifesto §8):** Squad agents value survival over slaughter. Critically wounded or low-poise agents execute emergent Tactical Retreats and earn permanent Crucible Scars rather than marching to scripted deaths.
- **Dream Intervention Mitigation:** Players can spend prescient Dream Siphon reserves to ward against catastrophic crises during real-time play or unattended Chrono-Skips.
